# Ticket 06 evidence: delay metric design (measured 2026-08-16, accepted by Ross the same day)

## Measured evidence

Archive: 2026-08-15 Sat 13:12-19:09 EDT continuous (17:12-23:09 UTC, 5.94 h), VP 30s / TU 120s;
1,522,799 unique pings, 3,121 vehicles, ~17.8K VP trips; 11.66M TU stop rows over 179 polls.
Static: current pick (2026-06-23/07-28), 32 service_ids active on the date, no calendar_dates exceptions.
Historical: nycbuspositions 2021-09-01 (Ida day), 1,311,872 unique pings, ~120 s poll cadence.
Script: `06-delay-metric-evidence.py` (v2, corrected after two adversarial reviews; reads the 2026-08-15 archive Parquet and the six static zips).

1. **RT trip_id joins the static GTFS trip_id exactly**: 98.4% (20,999/21,346). Every matched trip has an active service_id for the date (100%). Unmatched are `T430` shuttle-like route ids. Same trip_id scheme in the 2021 archive (`WF_C1-Weekday-033000_SBS6_153`).
2. **VP `stop_id` is the NEXT stop** (measured, not assumed; the spec's IN_TRANSIT_TO default does not apply because MTA omits `current_stop_sequence`): 94.9% of pings project between the previous static stop and their `stop_id` stop (median 39% of the way along the chord); the last ping before a flip is a median 71 m *before* the stop, the first ping after is a median 54 m *past* it. So a stop_id flip brackets the passage of the stop; the midpoint is the passage estimate, half-width = ping gap/2 (p50 15 s, p90 28 s, p99 41 s live; ~60 s in the 2021 archive).
3. **Passages** (monotone envelope on stop_sequence per (vehicle, trip), forward crossings only): 355,452; 94.2% advance one stop, 5.0% two, 0.8% three-plus (skipped stops need interpolation); 3.6% of raw flips are backward flaps (A-B-A) and are absorbed by the envelope. 16.9% of trip_ids are served by more than one vehicle_id in the window (reassignment / OBA double-publish): the passage key must include vehicle_id.
4. **Schedule delay** = passage - scheduled (noon-minus-12h rule on `start_date`): p10 -180 s, p50 +143 s, p90 +1,123 s; 22.9% early (< -60 s), 35.7% late (> 300 s). Same excluding first stops. Per-route p50 spans BX18B -169 s to M55 +1,323 s (M55 traced end to end: block cascade, TU agrees).
5. **Segment excess** (actual minus scheduled stop-to-stop time, both ends observed): p10 -55 s, p50 0 s, p90 +67 s, ratio p50 1.00 over 320,167 segments. A local, well-centred response variable, unlike delay (a cumulative level).
6. **TU is the prediction stream, VP is the arrival**: last TU prediction vs VP passage on the same (trip, stop), single-vehicle keys, n=333,452: p50 +18 s, 87.8% within 60 s, 97.2% within 120 s. Schedule delay on the same events: TU-last p50 172 s vs VP p50 147 s; late>5 min 38.2% vs 36.1%. TU-only stops (31% of completed series) are short (8 polls) and vanish with the prediction still ~10 min ahead: trip vanished, short-turned or reassigned, not arrivals.
7. **Prediction quality by horizon** (pred - passage): 0-2 min p50 +15 s, 98% within 2 min; 5-10 min p50 -5 s, 80%; 15-30 min p50 -68 s, 49%; 30-60 min p50 -149 s, 35%. A stop first appears p50 58 min ahead, gets ~28 polls and ~21 distinct predictions; churn range p50 301 s, p90 1,013 s. Fixed-horizon "delay" would be optimistic by 1-2.5 min and mostly wrong beyond 15 min; churn itself is a usable feature.
8. **Coverage**: 93.5% of scheduled trips in the window are seen in VP; 6.8% of trips scheduled fully inside the window never appear in VP (5.1% never in TU): the not-delivered/not-tracked share. Passages / scheduled non-terminal arrivals = 0.774 overall, 0.828 on seen trips; per-trip recall p50 0.88 before interpolating multi-stop jumps. Coverage must ride along as a column, never be assumed.
9. **Headway** (same-trip/same-vehicle followers excluded; 539 route-directions; scheduled headway p50 15 min; 29.7% <= 10 min, 48.4% <= 12, 62.5% <= 15 on a Saturday):
   - <= 10 min routes (135,545 headways): bunched (< 0.5x) 26.6%, under 2 min 15.1%, gapped (> 1.5x) 30.2%, wait_ok (<= sched + 3 min) 65.0%; per-stop EWT p50 3.9 min (SWT 4.6 -> AWT 8.5).
   - 10-15 min: bunched 19.3%, wait_ok 60.5%, EWT 4.8 min. > 15 min: bunched 9.4%, under 2 min 1.9%, wait_ok 62.9%, EWT 3.8 min.
   Bunching is a high-frequency phenomenon; excess wait is everywhere.
10. **Service day**: `trip.start_date` populated 100% (VP, TU, and `trip_start_date` in the 2021 archive; 13.6% of Ida-day pings belong to the previous service day). Static stop_times run to 28:xx; 2.1% of trips start after 24:00. Trip_id prefix is the depot code (EN, GH, MQ...), the 6-digit token is origin time in centiminutes (135000 = 22:30); neither marks the service day. The noon-minus-12h rule is the GTFS rule and is DST-safe only if implemented as noon anchor + timedelta (needs a unit test on 2024-03-10 / 2024-11-03).
11. **Backfill archive** (nycbuspositions) has NO stop-level TU rows (trip-updates CSV = trip headers only) and positions at ~120 s cadence with stop_id + IN_TRANSIT_TO. VP passages work there (906,790 on Ida day); TU features do not exist there. Dated static GTFS for 2017-2024 exists on Transitland (ticket 11).
12. **MTA's published metrics** (validation targets, verified definitions): Wait Assessment `v4z4-2h6n` = % of trips no more than 3 min over scheduled interval, per route/month/peak-offpeak, all routes; Customer Journey `8mkn-d32t` = Additional Bus Stop Time, Additional Travel Time, CJTP (% customer journeys within 5 min of scheduled), customer-weighted, by trip type EXP/LCL-LTD/SBS.

## Decisions (all eight accepted as recommended, 2026-08-16)

1. **Arrival truth = VP passage.** For each (vehicle_id, trip_id, start_date): sort pings by time, keep the monotone envelope of static stop_sequence, and every forward advance of the envelope is a passage of the previous envelope stop; `pass_lo_ts` = last ping before, `pass_hi_ts` = first ping after, `arrival_ts` = midpoint, `censor_halfwidth_s` = (hi - lo)/2. Ping identity for dedupe is (vehicle_id, ts, stop_id, lat, lon); when the feed republishes a moved vehicle under a frozen `ts`, `fetched_at` is the time axis. Multi-stop advances (k > 1) get the intermediate stops interpolated proportional to cumulative shape distance and flagged `interpolated=true`, `interp_k`. First stop of a trip is `is_first` (its flip measures pull-out, not arrival); the last stop never yields a passage (`is_last`, arrival null in the backfill era, TU-only live). Identical on 2017-2024 archive and live feed.
2. **TU is the prediction stream, never silently the arrival.** Per event keep `pred_last_ts` (fallback arrival when there is no VP passage, always with `arrival_src` in {vp_passage, tu_last, interpolated}) and churn features `pred_first_horizon_s`, `pred_n_changes`, `pred_range_s`, `pred_err_10min_s`. Null in the backfill era. No fixed-horizon delay metric.
3. **`delay_s` = arrival_ts - sched_ts**, sched_ts = local noon of `start_date` (America/New_York) - 12 h + stop_times.arrival seconds, from the static pick in effect on `start_date` (`static_pick_id` stored per row). Service date is the feed's `trip.start_date` (`trip_start_date` in the archive), never the poll clock; trips with no static match keep the arrival row with null sched (coverage-counted, not dropped); `schedule_relationship` stored, CANCELED filtered, ADDED/DUPLICATED flagged. Positive = late. Late = delay_s > 300, early = delay_s < -60 (on-time-performance convention; not a CJTP analogy), applied at Gold, not baked into Silver.
4. **The rain response variable is local, not cumulative**: `segment_s` = arrival_ts - previous stop's arrival_ts on the same (vehicle, trip), `sched_segment_s` likewise, `segment_excess_s` = difference. `delay_s` stays as the level ("how late is the bus"), `segment_excess_s` is what gets attributed to the H3 cell of the stop and joined to rain. Dwell is not separable at 30 s cadence (`pass_hi - pass_lo` is a weak proxy); named limitation.
5. **Headway columns everywhere, family flag only for the headline.** Every event gets `headway_obs_s` (previous different-vehicle arrival at the same route/direction/stop), `headway_sched_s`, `wait_ok` (obs <= sched + 180 s, MTA WA rule), `bunched` (obs < 0.5 sched). Gold computes EWT per (cell, hour, route) as AWT - SWT with the renewal formula on both sides (E[h^2]/2E[h]), never sched/2. `family` = headway where scheduled headway on that route-direction-hour <= 10 min (TCQSM cut; TfL uses 12; MTA uses none), else schedule; the flag decides which metric a Gold table headlines, nothing else.
6. **Speed stays with ticket 10.** Ping-to-ping speed rules are the backfill ticket's; this ticket fixes the arrival/segment/headway event table speed sits beside.
7. **Silver event grain: one row per (start_date, trip_id, stop_sequence, vehicle_id)** with route_id, direction_id, trip_type, stop_id, stop lon/lat + h3, arrival_ts, pass_lo_ts, pass_hi_ts, censor_halfwidth_s, arrival_src, interpolated, interp_k, is_first, is_last, sched_ts, static_pick_id, delay_s, segment_s, sched_segment_s, segment_excess_s, headway_obs_s, headway_sched_s, wait_ok, bunched, family, schedule_relationship, pred_* (null pre-2024-09), n_vehicles_on_trip. Coverage columns at Gold: `arrivals_obs / arrivals_sched`, `vp_coverage`, per cell-hour.
8. **Validation on MTA's own denominators**: per route x month x peak/off-peak, `wait_ok` share (all routes, timepoint stops if MTA's are known, else all stops) vs `v4z4-2h6n`; mean positive `delay_s` at stops vs `8mkn-d32t` additional_bus_stop_time by trip type. Tolerance set tighter than the VP-vs-TU estimator spread (about 2 points on late share); a first month is a calibration, not a pass/fail.

## Provisional (6 h of one Saturday, not the week the ticket asked for)
The 10-min family cut, the 300 s threshold, and the headway shares should be re-checked on a weekday and across a midnight crossing once the archiver has run a week; the rules above do not change, only the reported shares.
