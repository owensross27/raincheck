Ground and points

The bus route lines are 21,868 Cell crossings and 7.8 MB; the flood zones are 4.4 MB. Neither is fetched until you tick it.

The flood-history markers are 8,146 assets and 1.5 MB, fetched once when first ticked — never at boot. Clicking a marker fetches that one asset’s record alone (median ~1 KB, at most 22 KB).
